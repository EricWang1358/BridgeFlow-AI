# Ready-mix concrete supply contract (standard template, simulated sample A)

> Fictional sample written along the lines of a common ready-mix supply contract. For demonstration only; not a legal text.

## Article 1 Subject and quantity
The supplier delivers ready-mix concrete according to the buyer's written or telephone delivery plans. The provisional total volume follows the enquiry record; settlement follows the delivery notes signed by both parties.

## Article 2 Price
2.1 Unit prices are delivered prices including 13% VAT, transport and pumping.
2.2 Price list: C30 390 CNY/m³; C35 405 CNY/m³; C40 430 CNY/m³. The price confirmed in the quotation prevails.
2.3 Adjustment: when the delivered cement price moves more than 5% from the signing month, the unit price is adjusted pro rata to the cement content per cubic metre, effective from the following month.

## Article 3 Quantity confirmation and settlement
3.1 The volume on delivery notes signed on site is the basis of settlement; differences between dispatched and received volumes are reconciled on the same day.
3.2 Before the 25th of each month both parties reconcile the previous month's deliveries and sign a settlement statement.

## Article 4 Payment
4.1 The buyer pays the agreed proportion of the settlement amount by the end of the following month; the proportion is stated in the quotation.
4.2 For late payment the buyer pays a penalty of 0.03% of the overdue amount per day; if payment is more than 60 days overdue the supplier may suspend deliveries.

## Article 5 Quality
GB/T 14902 Ready-mixed Concrete applies; strength is determined by 28-day standard-cured specimens, and disputes are referred to a testing body accepted by both parties.

## Article 6 Other
If raw material prices fluctuate abnormally within the quotation's validity, the supplier may request a new quotation in writing.
