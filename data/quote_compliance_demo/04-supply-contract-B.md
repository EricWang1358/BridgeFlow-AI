# Ready-mix concrete supply contract (Hi-tech Talent Apartments Phase 2, simulated sample B)

> Fictional sample: a project contract supplied by the customer. For demonstration only; not a legal text.

## Article 1 Subject and quantity
The supplier supplies C35 pumped ready-mix concrete to the buyer, provisional total volume 4000 m³, settled on delivery notes signed by both parties.

## Article 2 Price
2.1 Unit prices are delivered prices including 13% VAT, transport and pumping; the price confirmed in the quotation prevails.
2.2 Unit prices are fixed for the term of the contract.

## Article 3 Payment
3.1 The buyer settles monthly and pays 70% of the month's settlement amount within 45 days of signing the settlement statement.
3.2 After completion acceptance the buyer pays up to 97% of the total settlement; the balance is retained as warranty money.

## Article 4 Liability for breach
4.1 If the supplier delivers late or the quality is nonconforming, it pays liquidated damages of 2% of the contract total per occurrence, capped in aggregate at 30% of the contract total.
4.2 Each party's total liability for damages under this contract is limited to the contract total.

## Article 5 Quality and warranty
5.1 GB/T 14902 Ready-mixed Concrete applies; strength is determined by 28-day standard-cured specimens.
5.2 The warranty period is 24 months from acceptance of the main structure; when it expires without quality disputes, the buyer pays the retained warranty money within ten working days.

## Article 6 Intellectual property
Intellectual property in the mix designs, test reports and technical documents provided by the supplier belongs to the supplier; the buyer may use them only for this project and may not disclose them to third parties.
